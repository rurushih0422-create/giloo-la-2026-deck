Beyond Festivals and Streamers

How films find audiences, unlock value, and drive impact — and what AI is about to change.

超越影展與串流：影片如何找到觀眾、釋放價值、創造影響力——以及 AI 即將改變的事。

Start in Taiwan

Real stories can fill theaters.

Let's start in Taiwan. Taiwan has a unique culture — we have a massive market for reality. Here, documentaries can fill major commercial theaters. For example, Beyond Beauty brought in over 7.5 million dollars at the box office. And recently, A Chip Odyssey made over 1 million dollars in theaters.

在台灣，紀錄片能坐滿大戲院。《看見台灣》票房逾 750 萬美元（約 2.2 億台幣），是台灣紀錄片史上票房冠軍；近期《造山者》接續在院線上映，票房逾百萬美元（3,600 萬台幣）。

Why documentaries work in Taiwan

Because issues bring people to theaters.

When a film talks about a serious social issue, audiences don't just watch it alone. They organize group screenings. They bring their friends. They show up together as a community.

因為議題驅動人們走進戲院。當一部電影談論嚴肅的社會議題，觀眾不會只是自己一個人看。他們發起包場、揪朋友一起來，作為一個社群一同現身。

giloo
Data insights

Data told us what they watch; community told us why it matters.

When we looked at our data, it showed something very different. Everyone thought people only wanted short videos and quick content. But in reality, our most-watched films focus on serious social issues, cultural trauma, and underrepresented communities.

數據告訴我們觀眾看了什麼，社群告訴我們這為何重要。回看數據，呈現的完全不同：大家以為觀眾只想要短影音與速食內容，但實際上，我們觀看量最高的電影，聚焦在嚴肅的社會議題、文化創傷與較少被看見的群體。

Issue-driven cinema · 01

Let's look at some of our top titles. Island in Between, directed by Leo Chiang.

Island in Between

金門 · 2023 · dir. S. Leo Chiang · Academy Award nominee, Best Documentary Short

Kinmen is a small island between Taiwan and China. In this film, Leo returns to Kinmen to look at his own identity, his family story, and where Taiwan stands in the world. And it became Taiwan's first Oscar-nominated documentary.

《金門》
poster

《金門》：一座位於台灣與中國之間的小島。導演江松長回到金門，凝視自己的身分、家族故事，以及台灣在世界上的位置。台灣首部入圍奧斯卡的紀錄片。

Issue-driven cinema · 02

Another example is And Miles to Go Before I Sleep, directed by Tsai Tsung-lung.

And Miles to Go Before I Sleep

九槍 · 2022 · dir. Tsai Tsung-lung · Golden Horse Award, Best Documentary

A real case in Taiwan: a Vietnamese migrant worker was shot and killed by police — shot nine times. The film had a strong impact, especially in schools, where teachers used it to help students understand migrant workers, labor rights, and basic human rights. Often, we are afraid because we don't understand — and film can help us build that understanding.

《九槍》
poster

《九槍》：本片講述一起真實發生在台灣的案件——一名越南籍移工遭警方槍擊身亡，中了九槍。這部片在台灣造成很大迴響，尤其在校園：許多老師把它當作教材，幫助學生理解移工、勞工權益與基本人權。很多時候，我們因為不了解而恐懼；而電影，能幫我們建立那份理解。

Issue-driven cinema · 03

And the third example is Black Box Diaries.

Black Box Diaries

黑箱日記 · 2024 · dir. Shiori Itō · Academy Award nominee, Best Documentary Feature

A journalist shares her story after sexual assault. The film looks at justice, courage, and the fight to tell the truth. It was not available to audiences in Japan, but it found a powerful response in Taiwan — connecting with Taiwan's own MeToo trauma, and opening a deeper conversation about how society often questions survivors instead of supporting them. It asks a difficult question: when someone speaks about sexual violence, are we willing to listen, and are we willing to believe them?

《黑箱日記》
poster

一名記者在遭受性侵後站出來訴說自己的故事，凝視正義、勇氣與說出真相的抗爭。本片在日本無法上映，卻在台灣得到強烈迴響。它連結了台灣自身的 MeToo 創傷，並打開更深的對話：社會往往質疑倖存者，而非支持他們。

What we saw on Giloo

Different stories. Same behavior.

These films are very different. They come from different countries and talk about different issues. But what we saw on Giloo was the same behavior. After people watched these films, the conversation continued on social media. They shared their reactions, they discussed the issues, and they brought more people back to Giloo to watch. That is what Giloo is built for.

不同的故事，一樣的行為。這些電影非常不同，來自不同國家、談論不同議題。但我們在 Giloo 上看到的是同一種行為：人們看完這些電影後，對話在社群媒體上延續——他們分享感受、討論議題，並帶更多人回到 Giloo 觀看。這正是 Giloo 存在的意義。

After seeing these amazing case studies, the question for us became

How do we make this happen

We realized that issue films don't create momentum by themselves. They are driven by people — by communities, trusted voices, and partners who already care about the issue.

於是我們的問題變成：要怎麼讓這件事一再發生？我們發現，議題電影不會自己產生動能——它們是被「人」推動的：被社群、被值得信任的聲音，以及那些本來就在意這個議題的夥伴。

So we built a way

We find the people who can move the films.

Once we find the right film, we don't just put it online and wait. We look for the people who can help the film travel — NGOs, influencers, and industry partners. Each of them brings a different kind of audience into Giloo.

NGOs

Groups that care about an issue can build film lists and share them with their communities.

Influencers

Trusted voices can introduce films to the people who follow them.

Industry partners

Festivals, cultural groups, and other film partners can build programs on Giloo.

找到對的電影後，我們不會只是把它放上線然後等待。我們會去找能幫這部片傳播的人——NGO、影響力者與產業夥伴，他們各自把不同的觀眾帶進 Giloo。

What we learned

Three things we learned from Giloo.

01 · People still matter

A real person choosing the right film can do more than an algorithm.

02 · People pay for meaning

Audiences don't just pay for content. They pay for stories and issues that matter to them.

03 · Community moves films

A film can travel further through real communities than through paid ads.

我們學到的三件事：① 人依然重要——真人挑對的電影，勝過演算法；② 人們為意義付費——觀眾付費不只為內容，更為對他們重要的故事與議題；③ 社群帶動電影——電影透過真實社群，比付費廣告走得更遠。

The problem worth solving

It's not a content problem. It's a distribution problem.

Great films are made every year. But many of them never reach the people who would love them. For independent filmmakers, it is still hard to translate, promote, and distribute their work across markets.

每年都有偉大的電影誕生，但許多從未觸及那些會愛上它們的人。對獨立導演而言，要把作品翻譯、推廣、跨市場發行，至今仍然困難。

Beyond festivals and streamers

Issue films need a better way to reach the right people.

Theaters

Powerful, but hard to enter and limited by location.

Festivals

Good for awareness, but the window is short.

Streamers

Built for scale, but small films get lost.

議題電影需要一條更好、能精準觸及對的人的路。戲院——有力量，但門檻高、受地點限制；影展——利於打開知名度，但曝光窗口短；串流——為規模而生，但小片容易被淹沒。

The wall we hit

The limit of doing it by hand.

If we do this only by hand, we can only help a small number of films each year — a few cities, a few partners, a few communities. But there are thousands of great filmmakers around the world with powerful films, and many of them don't have the team, the language support, or the marketing strategy to reach the people who would care.

如果只靠人力，我們每年只能幫上少數幾部電影，只能觸及幾座城市、幾個夥伴、幾個社群。但全世界有成千上萬優秀的導演與有力量的作品，他們之中許多人並沒有團隊、語言支援或行銷策略，去觸及那些會在意的人。

Over the past seven years, Giloo learned how to find the right films, the right issues, the right communities, and the right voices to move them. 我們開始問自己一個根本的問題：身為一個科技平台，我們該如何規模化？過去七年，Giloo 學會了如何找到對的電影、對的議題、對的社群，以及能帶動它們的對的人。現在，透過 AI，我們能把 know-how 轉化成導演能自己使用的工具。這就是 Giloo 不只是一個平台的原因——它成為獨立電影的全新支持系統。

Why we exist

Here, a documentary can change the law.

Films that moved a nation

Beyond Beauty reshaped environmental policy; Twelve Nights helped change Taiwan's animal-welfare law. The hunger for reality is in our culture.

But the system broke

Commercial theaters favor mainstream hits — brilliant issue-driven films get pushed out after a single week.

在台灣，一部紀錄片能改變法律。《看見台灣》推動環境政策、《十二夜》促成動物收容法規改革——對真實的渴望，寫在我們的文化裡。但這個系統壞了：戲院只留給主流大片，優秀的議題片往往一週就被下架。這正是七年前我們創辦 Giloo 想解決的事。

This is what we do

Giloo starts from the issue — not the algorithm.

Since 2018, Giloo has been Asia's curation-led home for documentary, art-house, and issue-driven film. Every title is chosen, not fed.

We don't sort films by "Drama" or "Thriller" — we organize them around the ideas that matter to society.

Giloo 自 2018 年起，是亞洲以「策展」為核心的紀錄片、藝術電影與議題電影平台。我們不用「劇情片」「驚悚片」等商業類型分類，而是圍繞著對社會重要的議題來組織故事。每一部片是「被選出來」的，不是被演算法推送的。
